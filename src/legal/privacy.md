FitSaver (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Account Information:** When you create an account or join the beta waitlist, we may collect your email address and basic account identifiers.
- **Workout Content:** When you import workout content (such as Instagram or TikTok links), we process the URLs, captions, and related metadata to generate structured workout routines.
- **Communications:** If you contact us for support or feedback, we collect the information you provide in your messages.
- **Payment Information:** If you subscribe or make in-app purchases, payment processing is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not store your credit card or payment details.

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information:** We may collect information about your device, including device type, operating system version, and anonymous device identifiers.
- **Usage Data:** We collect information about how you use the Service, including features accessed, workouts imported, and time spent in the app.
- **Analytics:** We use analytics tools to understand how users interact with FitSaver in order to improve performance and user experience.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
- Convert imported workout links into structured routines
- Enable workout tracking, completion, and milestones
- Process subscriptions and transactions
- Send technical updates, onboarding messages, and support responses
- Monitor usage trends and app performance
- Detect, investigate, and prevent misuse or fraudulent activity
- Personalize and improve your experience

## Workout Content Processing

Your privacy regarding workout content is important to us:

- **Temporary Processing:** Workout links, captions, and metadata are temporarily processed to extract structured workout information.
- **No Public Sharing:** Imported workout data is private to your account and is not shared publicly.
- **AI Processing:** To structure workouts, FitSaver may send text (such as captions or extracted information) to AI service providers (e.g., OpenAI) for processing. This data is used solely to generate workout structure and is not used to train models without your consent.
- **Local Storage:** Completed workouts and progress data are stored locally on your device and in secure backend storage tied to your account.

## Social Media Links

When you import workout links from platforms like Instagram or TikTok:

- Fitsaver only processes the publicly available content of the link
- We do not and cannot process content from private accounts.
- We do not store your social media credentials

## Video and Image Processing

FitSaver may process videos or images only to the extent necessary to extract workout-related information:

- **No Facial Recognition:** FitSaver does not perform facial recognition, biometric identification, or face analysis.
- **No Permanent Media Storage:** Original videos or images are not permanently stored on our servers unless required for the Service.
- **No Training Use:** User-provided content is never used to train AI models.
